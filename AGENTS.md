# Contributor Operating Rules

1. Mobile-first and accessible by default.
2. Never hard-code health claims or regulated product eligibility in presentation components.
3. All member/business data comes from LeveLab Backend APIs.
4. All conversation runtime comes from Atendimento.Center / LIA contracts.
5. LIA must always be identified as an AI assistant.
6. Preserve locale and attribution through landing -> assessment -> chat -> WhatsApp.
7. Do not commit secrets or real personal data.
